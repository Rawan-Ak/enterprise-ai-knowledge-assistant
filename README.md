# enterprise-ai-knowledge-assistant
An enterprise AI knowledge assistant exploring RAG, security, evaluation, and production AI architecture
## 1. Problem Definition
### What problem are we solving?
In large enterprises, operational knowledge is distributed across many policies, procedures, runbooks, incident reports, and technical documents stored in different locations. Multiple document versions and access restrictions can make it difficult for employees to quickly identify the correct and most relevant information. During incidents or operational activities, this can result in employees spending significant time searching for information or relying on colleagues with previous experience.

### Who are we solving it for?
Our primary users are service delivery and operations teams responsible for resolving incidents and following operational procedures. These teams often work under time constraints and SLA/KPI expectations, so delays in finding reliable information can directly affect operational performance.

### What would a successful solution accomplish?
Help users find relevant information quickly.
Provide answers grounded in approved documentation.
Show the sources supporting each answer.
Reduce the time users spend searching across multiple knowledge sources.
Clearly indicate when reliable information cannot be found.

### Why is this worth solving?
Time spent searching for information can delay incident resolution and create inconsistent responses. Teams can become dependent on experienced employees who know where information is located, while outdated or conflicting documentation can increase operational risk. Improving access to reliable operational knowledge could therefore help teams make faster and more consistent decisions.
